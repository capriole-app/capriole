# The Capriole name and logo

Capriole's code is free under the [0BSD licence](LICENSE): use it, change it, sell it, with no
conditions. The licence covers the code. It does not cover the project's identity: the name
«Capriole», the logo, the symbol and the other brand artwork listed below. They identify the
project's own builds and site (capriole.app) and stay with the Capriole project.

## What is not under 0BSD

- the name «Capriole» used as the name of a product or service;
- the files in `ARTWORKS/` (logo, symbol, avatar, social images and their sources);
- the copies of that artwork in the apps: `apps/*/static/` (`favicon.*`, `logo-*`, `mark*`,
  `apple-touch-icon.png`, `og/`) and `apps/desktop/src-tauri/icons/`.

All rights in them are reserved.

## What you may do without asking

- Say that your software uses, is built on, or is a fork of Capriole («based on Capriole»).
- Use the name and logo, unchanged, to talk about the project: articles, reviews, talks, links to
  capriole.app or to this repository.
- Build and run this repository as it is, logo included, for yourself or inside your organisation.
- Self-host an unchanged release of the web app ([docs/SELF-HOSTED.md](docs/SELF-HOSTED.md)), on
  your own domain, as long as it does not pass itself off as capriole.app.

## What needs permission

- Distributing a modified version, or a service built on it, under the name «Capriole» or with its
  logo. A fork gets a name and an icon of its own: replace the files listed above and the name in
  the apps.
- Using the name or logo in a way that suggests the Capriole project made, checked or endorses
  something it did not.
- Altered versions of the logo or symbol, or names that can be mistaken for «Capriole».

To ask, write to hello@capriole.app.
